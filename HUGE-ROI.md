# Easy Agent — legitimate revenue growth plan

**Status:** Business plan, not guaranteed earnings or evidence of paid customers.
**Updated:** 2026-10-11 (Australia/Adelaide)

## Core offer
Offer a clearly scoped **A$2,950 one-time AI workflow sprint** to eligible business customers. Example deliverables: discovery call, one workflow automation, basic dashboard, documentation, handover, and a defined support period. Confirm scope and timelines before collecting payment. Quote GST correctly for the entity and circumstances.

Optional follow-on: a maintenance agreement with a written scope, deliverables, cancellation terms, and agreed recurring price. Never silently enroll customers.

## First five-client target
- **Five genuinely paying customers x A$2,950 = A$14,750 gross sales** if achieved; this is a target, not present revenue or profit.
- Start with an accessible niche: Australian professional services and SMBs with manual sales follow-up, appointment handling, or content-production bottlenecks.
- Create a live demo with synthetic data, a transparent scope sheet, an outcome-based case study only when permission is granted, and a verifiable business identity.
- Offer a paid discovery/sprint to qualified decision-makers with truthful capabilities and explicit terms.
- Use consent-based, relevant outreach that follows Australian Spam Act and privacy requirements. Provide an unsubscribe route for commercial electronic messages; do not use deceptive identities or undisclosed scraping.
- Track leads and conversations in a least-privilege CRM. Do not collect or sell unrelated personal information.

## 30-day execution sequence
**Days 1–3:** Publish a clear landing page: what Easy Agent delivers, an honest demo, A$2,950 package scope, exclusions, refund/support terms, contact channel, and invoice/payment process.

**Days 4–10:** Identify 30–50 strongly matched businesses through ordinary public research. Prioritize warm introductions, referral partners and permission-based contact. No bulk unsolicited messaging.

**Days 11–20:** Run qualification calls; propose one measurable workflow improvement per business. Use approved documents and real testimonials only.

**Days 21–30:** Deliver paid pilots, measure outcomes with customer consent, request feedback, and offer appropriately scoped support.

## KPI dashboard and unit economics
Record: qualified leads, opted-in conversations, booked calls, quotes, signed agreements, paid invoices, gross revenue, refunds, payment fees, delivery hours, direct costs and customer outcomes.

- Gross revenue = paid, non-refunded sales (ex-GST where appropriate).
- Contribution = revenue minus direct delivery, gateway, refund, and acquisition costs.
- ROI % = (incremental net benefit - campaign spend) / campaign spend * 100.
- Treat forecasts as scenarios; do not display them as deposits or earned income.

**Example only:** 5 sold sprints at A$2,950 = A$14,750 gross before GST treatment, platform fees, labor, tax, advertising and refunds.

## Customer payment controls
- Use only an authorized, identity-verified Stripe business account or equivalent provider.
- Test checkout/webhooks before switching to live mode. Verify paid status server-side, issue a receipt and comply with chargeback/refund rules.
- No fabricated charges, fake invoices, unauthorized transfers, stolen payments, anonymous evasion, hidden payouts, or promises of guaranteed financial returns.
- Require explicit approval before launching live checkout, charging customers, purchasing ads, or sending campaigns.

## Growth after product-market fit
Systematize successful projects into reusable templates, upsell legitimate ongoing support, request permitted referrals, and expand into higher-value verticals only after delivery quality and retention are established.
