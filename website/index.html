Automation is scoped to your approved processes. Customer-facing follow-ups can require explicit owner approval, and appointments must use verified availability.

We assess your website, inbox, calendar and CRM during discovery. Any unverified connection is identified before it is included in the final scope.

Third-party hosting, model usage, messaging and payment-processing fees are separately disclosed in the proposal.

Yes. Larger integrations, internal developer tools and sports technology are scoped and priced separately.
